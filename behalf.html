Behalf: an AI that calls customer care for you.

Tell it on WhatsApp what you need, in Hindi, English or both. It finds the right number, gets through the IVR, waits on hold, talks to the agent and comes back with proof that the job is actually done. You spend forty seconds. It spends the forty minutes.

01 The problem Persistence is a tax on the customer

Customer care in India is built to deflect, and it works. The company saves money every time you give up. The cost of persistence falls entirely on you: the hold music, the IVR loop, the third time you explain the same problem.

Companies are now putting AI on their side of the line: voice bots, chatbots, smarter IVRs. Many of these are tuned to keep you away from a human, because a call that never reaches an agent looks like a win on their dashboard. Soon every company will have an AI agent answering the phone. The customer is the only one without one.

02 Why now Three things changed in two years

Voice agents can hold a real phone call in Indian languages. Turn-taking, interruptions, Hinglish and noisy lines went from research problems to configurable parts of a voice stack. Production systems now answer hundreds of thousands of Indian customer calls a month at about a second of response time.

The minutes got cheap. The raw model and carriage cost of a voice minute is around two US cents, and hold time is cheaper still: while on hold the agent only needs to listen for a human voice, not think. A typical 20 to 30 minute support call costs Behalf tens of rupees.

Indians already delegate on WhatsApp. People book, pay and complain through chat every day. Asking an assistant to "handle this" in a voice note is a habit that already exists. Behalf just finishes the job.

03 How it works Ask, plan, call, verify, chase
Exhibit 3. One request, five steps
The green step is the product. Anyone can build an agent that makes a call. The value is knowing the job is really done.
01

Ask

A voice note or text on WhatsApp, in any mix of Hindi and English. Behalf asks only what it is missing.

02

Plan

The right number from a verified directory, the IVR path that reaches a human, and the details the agent will ask for.

03

Call

Navigates the menu, waits on hold, discloses that it is an AI, explains the issue and asks for a reference number.

04

Verify

Checks every claim against something real: a confirmation email, an SMS, a status page. "Processed" is not proof.

05

Chase

Schedules a check for anything promised later, and calls again if the refund, credit or technician never arrives.

It never pays, cancels or blocks anything without asking you first in the chat.
OTPs and PINs go from you to the company. Behalf patches you in, mutes itself and does not record that part.
It says it is an AI at the start of every call, and whose behalf it is calling on.
It shares only what the task needs. You see exactly what it will share before it dials.

The verification step comes from running voice AI on the other side of the line. The most expensive failure in customer support is a false "done": the bot or the agent says your refund is processed, the call ends happy, and nothing actually happened. Behalf treats every promise as a claim until it has proof, and anything that cannot be proved yet stays marked pending with a date to check again.

04 Wedge and business Start where the outcome is in rupees

Start with money owed to people: refunds for cancelled flights and trains, failed UPI and card transactions, e-commerce returns, wrong charges. These are the calls people avoid most, the outcome is a number, and the value of finishing is obvious. Broadband, telecom and appointments come next, on a subscription.

Pricing: first call free; success fee on money recovered; ₹99 a month for unlimited non-money tasks (outages, bookings, appointments). Call cost assumes about two US cents a minute of model and carriage while talking and much less while holding.

The long game is a network. Once a large share of a company's support calls arrive through Behalf as clean, verified requests ("PNR X7K2QP, cancelled by airline, refund not received"), the company is better off taking them through an API than through an IVR and a human. That flips Behalf from a caller into the channel: consumers stay free or cheap, and companies pay to resolve requests faster than their own call centre can.

05 What could kill it And the answer I would test first
Companies block AI callers

Some will try. But a caller with the PNR, the issue and the history ready is cheaper for them to serve than a frustrated human on the third call. Test: completion rate by company for the first 50 companies; prioritise the ones that resolve.

Identity and OTPs

Banks and telcos verify the customer on the call. Design: a 20-second patch-in where you speak to the company directly while Behalf is muted. The demo's card scenario shows it.

Regulation and consent

Calls must be disclosed as AI, and personal data needs clear consent under the DPDP Act. Design: disclose on every call, show exactly what will be shared before dialing, keep recordings only with permission.

One wrong action

Trust breaks the first time an agent cancels the wrong booking. Design: Behalf can ask, request and record, but anything irreversible needs a tap from you in the chat.

A big player copies it

Google, Truecaller or a telco could build the calling part. Moat: the IVR map of Indian companies, the playbook for each type of problem, and the record of which promises each company actually keeps. That data compounds with every call.

06 What I would measure Resolved, not called

The easy metric is calls completed. It is also the wrong one: a call can end politely and fix nothing. Behalf's north star is jobs resolved with proof, checked against an email, an SMS, a status page or the money arriving. Around it: minutes of your time saved, rupees recovered, the share of promises that were kept on the date given, and whether people come back with a second job within 30 days.

First 90 days: 100 users, refunds only, three airlines and two e-commerce platforms. Measure resolved-with-proof rate and time to resolution against people doing it themselves. If Behalf can recover money people had given up on, the rest of the product follows.

07 Why I would build this From the other side of the line

I lead CX and AI product at ixigo, where a voice AI I own answers more than 750,000 customer calls a month. I know where IVRs dead-end, where agents stall, how Hinglish and noisy lines break speech recognition, and why "your refund has been processed" is the most dangerous sentence in customer service. Behalf is that knowledge pointed the other way: an agent that works for the customer.
